# Legislative Brief — Aklatan+

### A Hybrid Community Library System for Filipino Readers

*Prepared for the Office of the Senator · 2026-07-20*

---

**Aklatan+** is a homegrown digital platform that helps public libraries serve their communities by bringing their **physical and digital collections together in one place** — a modern reading and lending service that runs on the phones and low-cost devices Filipinos already own. It is built to **support and align with the National Library of the Philippines (NLP)**, not to replace it.

## The problem

Access to books and learning materials remains uneven across the country. Many public and school libraries have limited digital capacity, physical collections are hard to discover, and licensed eBooks — where they exist — are difficult to lend securely. The result is that students, teachers, and community members in less-served areas have fewer opportunities to read and learn than those in well-equipped urban centers.

## What Aklatan+ does

- **Unifies physical and digital collections** so a reader can discover, borrow, and return both printed and electronic titles through one simple app.
- **Lends licensed eBooks securely** — each digital loan is individually access-controlled and time-limited, so libraries can offer eBooks without enabling unauthorized copying.
- **Works on low-cost devices** and is designed to remain usable on modest connections, so the least-served readers are not left behind.
- **Keeps reader data in the country and in the library's hands** — borrowing and reading history is treated as confidential, and libraries retain ownership of their catalog and member records.

## Why it fits national priorities

- **Data sovereignty & no vendor lock-in.** Aklatan+ stores its data in a standard, open database (PostgreSQL) that can be self-hosted and fully exported at any time. Records already export into the **Koha** open-source library system used across the Philippine public-library community — proof the data stays portable and standards-aligned.
- **Strengthens, not sidelines, physical libraries.** The platform is explicitly designed to reinforce the role of local libraries and to seek NLP's guidance and standards, contributing to national modernization rather than competing with it.
- **Affordable and sustainable.** It is built to be low-cost to run and maintainable by a library's own staff over the long term — a practical contribution for public libraries, schools, and local government units.
- **Privacy and copyright built in.** Reader privacy and content licensing are enforced by the system's design, consistent with the trust a public library is expected to uphold.

## The ask

We respectfully seek the Office's **endorsement and support for a limited, low-cost pilot** in one cluster of NLP public libraries (proposed: the National Capital Region cluster). The pilot is light-touch, uses libraries' existing collections and devices, and is structured to produce clear, measurable results that inform any wider rollout. A companion **Pilot Brief** and **Technical Dossier** are available on request.

## Safeguards & cost

The pilot carries modest operating costs and no long-term lock-in. Reader records remain confidential and library-owned; every eBook loan is access-controlled; and all administrative actions are logged for accountability. Aklatan+ is offered in a spirit of partnership with the NLP and the wider library community.
