Dear Sir/Madam,

I respectfully seek the National Library's guidance on **Aklatan+**, a Hybrid Community
Library System I have been developing to help public libraries modernize by integrating
traditional operations with digital library technology — **while preserving the vital role
of physical libraries**. The concept was first shared with a local government unit as an
enhancement to its e-library initiative; it was not pursued there, and that experience is
what led me to seek the National Library's guidance instead, to ensure any further work
aligns with national standards and direction.

## What Aklatan+ offers

- Unified management of physical and digital collections
- Mobile and web access for library patrons, with a **home-library** affiliation so a
  national platform still feels local
- Secure digital borrowing of eBooks and learning materials, with **in-app reading**
- Automated notifications for due dates and material availability
- Borrowing history and member account management
- Library analytics and administrative reporting
- A scalable architecture adaptable by public libraries, schools, and LGUs

## Where it stands today

This is not a paper concept. A working cross-platform build already exists — a web app and
a mobile app sharing one design system and one secure cloud backend — with a member
dashboard (browsing, borrowing, continue-reading, due-soon alerts, notifications) running
on both. It is governed by five fixed principles: **accessibility, inclusivity, purposeful
innovation, security & privacy by design, and community service & sustainability**.

## Posture: complement, not replace

Aklatan+ does not seek to replace the National Library, the Philippine eLib, or any
partner institution or local library's system of record. It is offered as a practical
contribution that **carries forward the eLib's 2004 vision** — access to information for
life-long learning, enriched local content, a nationwide library network — with today's
technology, in cooperation with the institutions that have long advanced it. Local
libraries retain ownership of their collections, catalog data, and reader relationships.

Should the National Library ever see reason to adopt Aklatan+, that decision is designed to
be a small, reversible step: the build already works, a pilot needs no procurement decision
to start, no library's existing catalog/ILS is disturbed, and every phase ends in a review
gate the National Library controls.

## The guidance I am seeking

1. **Feedback and recommendations** on how Aklatan+ can best align with the NLP's
   standards, existing initiatives (including the Philippine eLib), and long-term
